1. Sample fabrication
    The master alloy of Au49Cu26.9Ag5.5Pd2.3Si16.3 (at.%) was first melt using arc
melting furnace. Metallic glass ribbon was obtained by the melt spinning technique
(tangent speed ~40 m/s). During melting, the alloy was protected by high-purity Ar. A
tiny sample was cut from the ribbon under microscope and loaded on the flash DSC
chip for measurement.

5.   The absolute reaction rate theory

reaction peak position (Tp) versus heating rate Rh was build [1]. The form of structure
defects of crystal, such as vacancy defects, can contribute to the formation enthalpy
and the formation entropy. That is to say, the formation free energy ∆G=∆H-T∆S.
However, the metallic glasses contain lower packing and higher packing density and
some results also shown that the cooperative atomic motion in relaxation and
diffusion processes [2-4]. Therefore, the activation behavior of the cooperative atomic
motion can not contribute to the formation enthalpy and the formation, they can
contribute to the migration enthalpy and migration entropy [2]. There, we analyze the
internal stress σ (units: Pa) change with time during annealing process upon an
